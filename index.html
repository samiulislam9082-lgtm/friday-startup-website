    Questions

    Good to know.

    More answers in our help centre.

    Do I need all 18 services?

    No. Most businesses start with one or two, often an AI Receptionist or a new website, and add more as they see results.

    Will the AI assistants sound like my business?

    Yes. We set them up with your services, prices, opening hours and tone of voice, and agree what they may and may not say.

    What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

    SEO helps you rank on Google. AEO helps you become the direct answer in answer boxes and voice assistants. GEO helps AI chat tools understand and recommend your business.

    Can it work with the tools I already use?

    In most cases. CRM setup, business automation and custom dashboards are designed to connect your existing tools rather than replace them.
