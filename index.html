The idea behind Friday Startup

Build ideas. Make work better.

Friday Startup is an AI company exploring two connected directions: AI innovation, including new digital products and ventures, and business automation for organizations. These are intended directions, not a list of products or outcomes already delivered.

Current public service descriptions are venture building, growth partnerships and business recovery support. They are separate from the future AI direction. No specific customer engagement, AI product, result or metric is announced here.

An intended way of working

Start with context. Move with care.

Each idea and business is different. Friday Startup’s stated approach is to understand the situation before choosing what to build or test.

01 / Understand

Listen to the people and the problem.

Learn what matters to customers, teams and the business before making assumptions about a solution.

02 / Focus

Choose one useful next question.

Define a product idea or workflow opportunity that can be considered in a clear, bounded way.

03 / Learn

Test, learn and decide.

Use an appropriate pilot or prototype to learn what should continue, change or stop. No outcome is promised in advance.
