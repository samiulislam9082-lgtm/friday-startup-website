Our core service

Business Agents: one connected team for marketing, website, social media and growth.

Instead of juggling separate tools and freelancers, you get Business Agents set up around how your company works. They handle the repetitive work across your channels, keep everything in sync and bring the important decisions back to you.

Business Agent Your company hub

Marketing Agent

Keep your marketing consistent without doing it all by hand.

  • Plans campaigns and content around your goals
  • Drafts emails, offers and announcements for your review
  • Follows up with new leads and enquiries
  • Tracks what's working so the next campaign is sharper

Website Agent

A website that stays current, fast and ready for customers.

  • Keeps pages, offers and business details up to date
  • Publishes news, updates and new content
  • Routes website enquiries to the right person
  • Watches for broken pages and performance issues

Social Media Agent

Show up on every platform, on schedule and on brand.

  • Prepares and schedules posts across your platforms
  • Keeps your voice and visuals consistent
  • Flags comments and messages that need a reply
  • Summarises engagement in plain language

Growth Agent

Connect everything so your business can keep growing.

  • Brings marketing, website and social activity into one view
  • Spots repetitive work that can be automated next
  • Prepares regular reports for decision-making
  • Helps you test new channels and ideas with care

Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions.

What we do, how Business Agents work, and how to reach us.

What are Business Agents?

Business Agents are our core service. We set up automation that supports your marketing, website management, social media and growth work, scoped around your workflows. Your team stays involved in approvals and decisions that need judgment.

What services does Friday Startup offer?

Friday Startup offers Business Agents and business automation, venture building, growth partnerships and business recovery support. We are also developing more innovative solutions, including future AI innovation.

Does Friday Startup guarantee financial or growth outcomes?

No. We do not make generic promises of cost reduction, customer acquisition volumes or turnaround success. Real outcomes depend on business context, market conditions and collaborative execution.

Can founders and companies worldwide get in touch?

Yes. Friday Startup welcomes conversations with founders and operating businesses globally. Scope, schedules and availability are confirmed directly by email or phone.
