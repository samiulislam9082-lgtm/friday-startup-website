    Help

    Questions, answered.

    Can't find what you need? Visit the Help Center or get in touch.

    What are Business Agents?

    Business Agents are our core service. We set up automation that supports your marketing, website management, social media and growth work, scoped around your workflows. Your team stays involved in approvals and decisions that need judgment.

    What services does Friday Startup offer?

    Business Agents and business automation, venture building, growth partnerships and business recovery support. We're also developing more innovative solutions, including future AI innovation.

    Do you guarantee financial or growth outcomes?

    No. We don't make generic promises about cost savings, customer numbers or turnaround success. Real outcomes depend on your business context, market conditions and how we work together.

    Do you work with companies worldwide?

    Yes. We welcome conversations with founders and operating businesses globally. Scope, schedules and availability are confirmed directly by email or phone.
