04 / Business recovery support

Facing losses? Start with a clear diagnosis.

When a business is under pressure, we can work with the team to understand what is happening and shape a practical, evidence-led path forward. Options may include digital transformation, product opportunities, process improvements, and small, measurable pilots—chosen to fit the situation.

No turnaround or profit is guaranteed. The right next step depends on the business, what the assessment shows, and what can be tested.

07 / A few useful answers

Before we get started.

A little context for product founders and teams looking for a growth partner.

What can Friday Startup help with?

We build and launch digital ventures under the Friday Startup brand, and can partner with other companies on product, positioning, acquisition experiments, and customer journeys.

Do you work with companies outside your own ventures?

Yes. Growth partnerships are a separate way to work with teams who want a considered next step for an existing business.

Can you help a business that is dealing with losses?

We can work with the team to assess what is driving the challenge and explore a practical recovery path, which may include digital transformation, product opportunities, process improvements, and measurable pilots. We do not guarantee recovery or profits; the next steps depend on the business and what the evidence supports.

How do we start a conversation?

Use the Gmail button in the contact section below. It opens a new Gmail message addressed to Friday Startup.

Where will upcoming products be announced?

Scheduled product previews appear in Launch Radar 48 hours before their launch date. At launch, the product mark moves into the venture lineup.
