Business recovery support questions

Clear scope, no assumed outcome.

Is recovery guaranteed?

No. Friday Startup does not guarantee recovery, profit, turnaround or any other business outcome.

Is this insolvency or debt restructuring advice?

This page does not offer regulated insolvency, legal, financial or debt restructuring advice. Obtain qualified professional advice where the situation requires it.

Can businesses worldwide get in touch?

Yes. Friday Startup welcomes business discussions worldwide. Scope, delivery arrangements and any legal or market limits must be confirmed directly.

What should I share first?

Email a short description of the business challenge and the decision you are trying to make. Avoid sharing confidential records before an appropriate arrangement is agreed.
