Growth Partnership FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Does Friday Startup guarantee specific sales numbers or revenue?

No. Customer acquisition and revenue outcomes depend on market readiness, execution, pricing, and external competition. We commit to rigorous, evidence-based experimentation rather than unrealistic marketing hype.

How are growth engagements structured?

We work in bounded sprint cycles focused on specific hypotheses—such as testing a new go-to-market message, auditing a conversion funnel, or restructuring onboarding workflows.
