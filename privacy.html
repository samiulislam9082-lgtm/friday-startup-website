Website information

Privacy notice draft.

Owner review required. This is a draft for the local redesign review, not legal advice or an approved production privacy policy. Confirm the final hosting, font and data practices before publication.

What this review build does

This static review build has no contact form, analytics script, advertising pixel or cookie based tracking. It does not create an account or store visitor submitted data.

Font request

The design currently requests the Manrope typeface from Google Fonts. A font request can share technical request information with that provider. Before publishing, Friday Startup can keep this font request or use a locally hosted font file after confirming its licence and maintenance.

Links to other services

The official Gmail, Facebook, Instagram and X links take visitors to those services. Their providers handle information under their own policies. This website does not control what happens after a visitor leaves it.

Hosting records

A production host may create technical access logs. The actual log contents, retention period and access controls depend on the selected hosting configuration and should be confirmed before launch.

Email

If you contact Friday Startup at fridaystartup1@gmail.com, the email exchange is handled through the email services used by the sender and recipient. This static site does not keep a copy of the message.

Before production

Review this draft against the final hosting, analytics, consent and contact setup. If a tracker, form or other data collection is added later, update the notice and obtain any consent required before it is used.
